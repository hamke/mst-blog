1. Introduction to KoreaTalk Online Community Services

“KoreaTalk“ is a message-board-style online community where people from around the world who live in Korea—or are interested in life in Korea—can freely share information.

KoreaTalk operates separate services based on users’ country or region of origin and language. (The service list is updated regularly.)

3. How to Use KoreaTalk Community Services and Important Notes

All KoreaTalk community services are open to everyone because registration does not require users to enter their current region of residence, country of origin, or similar information.

If you offer services such as tourism or dining, or sell products to foreign residents and visitors in Korea, you can promote them by posting on the “Business & Service Promotions” board of the relevant regional or language community.

If you wish to recruit foreign residents living in Korea, you can also reach potential applicants effectively by posting on the “Recruit Info” board of the relevant regional or language community.

Posts should generally be written in the primary language of each website. Please also note that content involving political or diplomatic disputes, religion, or other illegal subject matter may be automatically filtered or blacklisted.
